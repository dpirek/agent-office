Methodology

This report was assembled at 15:00 UTC on Sept. 7, 2026 from publisher pages and Google News RSS results, prioritizing the previous 24–72 hours. Transparency & limits

Publisher article links are routed through Google News because some publisher pages blocked automated inspection; each link identifies its publisher and opens the indexed article. Reuters’ site returned an anti-bot challenge during research. AP’s hub was accessible but too large for complete extraction. This is a snapshot, not a live feed.
